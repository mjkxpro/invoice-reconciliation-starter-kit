Invoice-to-payment reconciliation exception checklist

When invoice exports and payment exports do not line up, use this short workflow to separate clean matches from items that need review.

Important: every example on this page is synthetic data, not a customer record. Do not upload personal, bank, or other sensitive information to public tools.

Five checks before you call a payment matched

SignalSuggested handling
Invoice reference and amount agreeMatched; retain the source references.
Amount is smaller than the invoiceFlag as underpaid or partial; do not silently close.
Amount is larger than the invoiceFlag as overpaid or combined; review allocation evidence.
No usable referenceSearch by date, currency, and amount, then flag as unmatched if uncertain.
One payment covers several invoicesRequire a documented allocation before marking the invoices settled.

Minimal review workflow

  1. Normalize invoice IDs, dates, currency, and amounts.
  2. Run exact reference-and-amount matching first.
  3. Review partial, lump-sum, duplicate, and missing-reference exceptions.
  4. Keep the source exports unchanged and record the reason for each decision.
